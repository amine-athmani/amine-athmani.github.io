Toolbox

Exact methods

MILP/MIP with CPLEX, Gurobi, HiGHS, CBC; constraint programming with CP Optimizer and OR-Tools CP-SAT; decomposition, warm starts, goal programming.

Heuristics at scale

Timefold, Hexaly, local search, simulated annealing, genetic algorithms, LAHC, GRASP, ACO for problems too large to prove optimal.

Optimization4All workshop, 2026

A live, hands-on workshop on one scheduling problem solved two ways: an exact MIP model in Pyomo with HiGHS, then a metaheuristic in Timefold.
